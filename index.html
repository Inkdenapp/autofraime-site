What it does

Shoot, finish and share a listing from one app.

Everything an agent needs to go from walkthrough to live listing, without handing photos off to anyone.

Auto Fraime camera

✨

AI Enhance

Bright, balanced exposure, true white walls, cloudy skies swapped for blue ones, TVs switched off, and people and pets removed. Built to keep the room exactly as it is.

How it works

Three steps, one sitting.

Shoot

Create a listing, pick the address, and walk the property. The camera keeps you level and straight as you go.

Enhance

Tap Enhance on a photo or Enhance All for the whole listing. It runs in the background, so you can keep shooting.

Share

Export for the MLS, send a client gallery, or lay out a flyer. Everything is backed up to your account.

FAQ

Questions agents ask.

Do I need a real camera?

No. AUTOFRAIME is built for the iPhone you already carry, including the ultra-wide lens for small rooms.

Will the AI change the house?

It is designed to keep the room, layout, windows, fixtures, finishes and artwork exactly as photographed, and to change light, color and clutter only. AI can still make mistakes, so review every photo before you list it, and follow your MLS rules on disclosing edited or virtually altered photos.

How long does an enhance take?

Usually about a minute, depending on your connection. It runs in the background, and you get a notification when it is done.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Photos are stored privately in your account. They are only visible to someone else if you choose to share a gallery link or export them. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, in the app under Account, Delete account. It permanently removes your account, every listing and photo, and your token balance.
